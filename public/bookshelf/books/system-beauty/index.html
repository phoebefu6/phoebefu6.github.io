THE BOOK IN 30 SECONDS

Behavior is not an accident.
It is the output of structure.

A system's stocks and flows create its state. Feedback loops shape change. Delays and nonlinearities hide cause and effect. Rules, goals, and paradigms determine direction. Lasting change starts when you stop blaming events and begin redesigning relationships.
